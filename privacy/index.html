Privacy Policy — DC Assistant

Last updated: 2026-10-28

DC Assistant is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, DC Assistant reads the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Sheets, and can save email drafts in his Gmail. It never sends email on its own.

How the data is used

  • The data is shown only to the owner, on his own computer.
  • It is not sold, shared, or sent to any other person or company.
  • It is not used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
